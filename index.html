How to play

Pick a piece from the toolbar, then click a square on the table to place it. A piece dropped next to an open track end (the green arrows) snaps on automatically at the right height and direction. Press Drop marble to release a marble from every start piece.

Keyboard shortcuts
Key Action
1–6 Choose a piece
X Erase tool
R Rotate the piece
Page Up / Page Down Raise or lower the build height
Arrow keys Move the build cursor (board focused)
Enter Place the piece at the cursor (board focused)
Delete Remove the piece at the cursor (board focused)
O Jump to the next open track end
M Drop a marble
P Pause or resume
S Turn sound on or off

To install Marble Run as an app, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Clear the board?

This removes every piece. It can’t be undone.
